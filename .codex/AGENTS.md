For full instructions, read `SKILL.md` in the project root.

trends, ingredients, brands, whitespace, category structure, or launch decisions.

`knowledge/patterns/cross-category-patterns.md` (what holds across categories),
`knowledge/categories/` and `knowledge/wellness/` (category detail).
